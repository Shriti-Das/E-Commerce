# E-commerce Business Analysis — Sales & Customer Retention

## 📌 Overview
Analyzed e-commerce transaction data to understand sales performance, customer behavior, and retention opportunities using a Business Analyst approach.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze revenue and sales trends
- Identify top products, countries, and customers
- Compare one-time vs repeat customers
- Analyze purchase frequency
- Segment customers using RFM-based rules
- Identify at-risk and high-value customers
- Provide actionable retention recommendations

## 🛠️ Tools
**SQL | MySQL | Python | Pandas | Excel | Power BI | Draw.io**

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard
- **Sales Overview:** Revenue, Orders, AOV, Top Products & Countries
- **Customer Analytics:** Repeat Customers, Purchase Frequency & Customer Revenue
- **Retention & Segmentation:** RFM Segments, At-Risk & High-Value Customers

## 💡 Business Outcome
Identified customer segments and purchasing patterns to support data-driven strategies for **customer retention and revenue growth**.
